## Disputes and remaining limits

**No disputes.** No fixture/spec exception or name/path-based allowance was added.
The dedicated AST/value-lattice architecture is retained; context recognition
uses createContext provenance rather than printed type names.

The finite corpus has no missed obligations; this is not a general soundness
proof. Remaining limits include a non-general heap/closure-cell model,
path-insensitive local updates, first-activation recursion, mixed/dynamic
defaults, no cross-project provider metadata and post-await scope precision.
See NOTES.md for the approach, assumptions and limits.
